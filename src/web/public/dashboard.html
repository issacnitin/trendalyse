01 / DISCOVER
Idea research starters

A fresh exploration can use two SerpApi search credits: one comparison chart and one related-query lookup. Cached repeats within an hour don't use new credits.

New to market research? Go from an idea to a practical test.

1. Pick a specific question

Choose an audience and need, like busy households looking for convenient food. Compare "meal prep" and "healthy snacks" rather than a broad category like "food."

2. Compare the signals

Compare related topics in your target country across 12 months and 5 years. Look for sustained interest, seasonality, and related searches.

3. Generate a hypothesis

Ask AI for market insights. Use its audience, search evidence, and validation step to frame one focused idea to test.

4. Validate before committing

Interview 5-10 people in your target audience. For a commercial idea, test willingness to pay with a small experiment. Trends alone don't establish demand.
